# User guide

This document explains how instructors, students, and admins use EvoPIE through
the web interface.

## Instructor workflow

Instructors organize work around courses, questions, and quizzes.

1. Create a course.
2. Create questions.
3. Add distractors to each question.
4. Create a quiz.
5. Attach questions and distractors to the quiz.
6. Attach the quiz to a course.
7. Add students to the course.
8. Release the quiz by status or by deadline.
9. Review grades and student work after students participate.

A question has a stem, a correct answer, and one or more distractors. A
distractor is a wrong but plausible answer choice. Distractors are important
because students explain why unselected alternatives are incorrect.

A quiz is built from questions and selected distractors. Instructors can choose
whether a quiz is controlled manually by status or automatically by deadlines.

## Student workflow

Students see available quizzes on the main page after logging in.

A quiz may move through these stages:

- Step 1
- Step 2
- Step 3
- Solutions

### Step 1

When a student first enters Step 1, EvoPIE creates an attempt for that
student. The quiz may require a step-specific passphrase.

In Step 1, students answer the quiz individually.

For each alternative they do not select, students write a justification
explaining why that alternative seems incorrect. These justifications are saved
for peer instruction in Step 2.

Students may reopen the quiz before submitting it. After submitting Step 1,
they wait until the instructor or deadlines move the quiz forward.

### Step 2

In Step 2, students revisit the quiz.

They can see selected justifications written by other students for each
alternative. EvoPIE selects these from justifications already submitted and
stores the selection on the attempt, so it stays the same when the student
returns to the page. Students may revise their answers after reviewing those
peer justifications.

Students can also like peer justifications. The quiz configuration sets how
many are shown, and the participation threshold determines the number of likes
needed for participation credit. Likes also contribute to justification-related
grading.

### Step 3

Step 3 is optional and only appears when enabled for the quiz.

In Step 3, students propose new distractors for questions from the quiz. They
also justify why each proposed distractor is a plausible wrong answer.

Instructors can later review and grade this distractor-design work.

### Solutions

In the Solutions stage, students can see the correct answers and instructor
justifications. If Step 3 was enabled, they may also see feedback on submitted
distractors.

## Grades

EvoPIE grades more than final correctness. Depending on quiz configuration, the
final grade may include:

- Step 1 correctness.
- Step 2 revised correctness.
- Participation through likes.
- Quality of received justifications.
- Step 3 distractor-design work.

Instructors can configure the weights of these components for each quiz.

## Data dashboard

The data dashboard gives instructors analysis views for quiz and student data.
The updater process refreshes the data used by those dashboard views.
